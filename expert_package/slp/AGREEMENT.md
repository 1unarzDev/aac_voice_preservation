# Expert services agreement: rating panel

This is a short professional-services agreement between the authors (the "Authors") and a speech-language pathologist (the "Reviewer")
for an expert review. It is not a research consent form: the Reviewer is paid for professional work, and no information about the
Reviewer is collected or studied.

**1. The work.** The Reviewer will review study materials: (a) rate the seriousness of seven kinds of meaning change in
machine-written sentences, and (b) rate 50 machine-written sentences for whether they say what the selected keywords mean and whether
they would be acceptable to speak as a client's message. Expected time: about 60 minutes, using the tool and instructions provided.
The subject of the work is the sentences, not the Reviewer.

**2. Payment.** The Authors will pay **[AMOUNT AND CURRENCY, to be filled in]** by **[PAYMENT METHOD]**, on return of the completed
file or, if the Reviewer withdraws first, for the time spent at the same rate pro rata. Payment does not depend on the content of the
ratings.

**3. No personal information.** The tool and the file collect only a reviewer ID (such as SLP1) and the ratings and optional notes the
Reviewer chooses to type. The Authors do not ask for demographics, health or disability information, information about the Reviewer's own
communication, clients or workplace, and no audio or video is recorded. The Reviewer agrees not to put personal or client details in notes. The
payment details needed to pay the Reviewer are kept separately from the ratings and are not published.

**4. Use of the ratings.** The Reviewer agrees that the ratings and notes may be used in the Authors' analysis and may be published
anonymously, in aggregate (for example, mean severity by type and agreement between reviewers), in papers, reports and datasets. Short
notes may be quoted without identifying the Reviewer. Acknowledgement by name is possible only if the Reviewer asks for it in writing.

**5. Withdrawal.** The Reviewer may stop at any time and may withdraw before submitting the file; in that case no ratings are kept.
After the file has been submitted it cannot be tied back to an individual, because it carries only an ID that the Authors cannot link to a
person outside payment records, so individual ratings cannot be removed from aggregates after submission.

**6. Independence.** The Reviewer works independently, does not use AI tools to decide ratings, and does not share the materials.
The Reviewer's ratings are professional opinions on the materials. They are not clinical advice and do not represent any employer.

**7. Authors' statements.** The Authors will describe the work in publications as an expert review by two speech-language pathologists,
not as a population estimate and not as an evaluation of any person or service.
