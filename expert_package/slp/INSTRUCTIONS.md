# Expert rating panel: instructions for speech-language pathologists

Thank you for agreeing to act as an expert reviewer. Please read this page and `AGREEMENT.md` before you start.

## Purpose
We are building and testing a writing assistant that turns a few keywords chosen by a person who uses AAC into a full sentence. The
risk we study is a *silent meaning change*: the sentence says something the person did not select (a dropped "not", a question that
becomes a statement, an added "please", and so on). We ask you, as a clinician, to judge **the sentences and the kinds of change**.
You are reviewing study materials. We are not asking about any person, and we do not collect information about you beyond a
reviewer ID.

## What you will do (about 60 minutes in total)
1. **Task 1, severity (about 15 minutes).** Seven kinds of silent change, each shown with two real examples. For each kind you rate how
   serious it would be if spoken as a client's message (1 trivial to 5 critical, with written anchors) and whether you would want a
   system to block or flag it.
2. **Task 2, acceptability (about 40 minutes).** 50 cards. Each shows the keywords or cues a person selected and a sentence a system
   wrote. You answer (a) *Does it say what the selected words mean?* (yes / partly / no) and (b) *Would it be acceptable to speak this as
   the client's message?* (yes / yes, with an edit / no). A note is optional. You will not be told which system produced a sentence.
3. About 5 minutes to open the file and finish. Take a break whenever you like. You may leave any question blank.

Both reviewers rate the same 50 cards. Please work alone and do not discuss cards with the other reviewer until both of you have returned your files.

## How to do it
1. Open `slp_rating.html` in a desktop browser (double-click). It works offline and needs no installation. It is built for keyboard and
   screen-reader use and has a text-size control.
2. Type the reviewer ID we gave you (`SLP1` or `SLP2`) in the box at the top. **Do not type your name, employer, clients or any personal
   details, in the ID box or in notes.** If a note would need such detail, leave it out.
3. Answers save automatically in your browser. Press **Download CSV** when you finish and again before you close the tab. To continue on
   another day, open the file again, enter the same ID and use **Resume from a CSV**.

## Anonymity
The only identifier is the reviewer ID. We collect no demographics, no questions about your own practice, and no recordings. We do not
ask where you work or who you have treated. Ratings may be published in aggregate and anonymously (see `AGREEMENT.md`). If you wish to
be acknowledged by name for expert review, tell us separately; this is optional and never required.
