# Feedback log (authors fill this in)

One row per suggestion, from both consultants. Do not record names. Use C1 and C2. Record the suggestion in neutral words, close to what was
said. No scoring and no statistics on consultant answers: this log is the only analysis. "Change" means a change to a requirement (R1 to R5),
a definition, an example, the chip or plain-word design, or the wording of the packet or papers.

Rules for the log:
- Every suggestion gets a row, including ones we do not follow.
- "Not made" always needs a reason. Reasons can be: out of scope, conflicts with another suggestion, already covered by (say where), needs a user
  study, needs a different system, disagree (explain).
- Record the date, and the version of the requirement before and after.
- The count N in the paper is the number of rows with Change made = yes that alter a requirement (R1 to R5) or add a new one. Other changes are counted
  separately as "design or wording changes".
