# Consulting agreement: design review

This is a short agreement for paid consulting between the authors (the "Authors") and a design consultant who uses AAC (the
"Consultant"). It is written in plain language. It is not a research consent form. The Consultant is paid for professional design advice
about a system and its requirements. The Authors do not study the Consultant.

**1. The work.** The Consultant reads a packet (`review.html`, or the print version `REVIEW_PACKET.md`) with five requirements for a
writing assistant, a description of a button (chip) idea and a plain-word idea, and ten example sentences. The Consultant gives feedback on
the requirements, the design idea and the examples. Expected time: 60 to 90 minutes, but there is **no time limit**. The Consultant may take breaks and
work over several sessions.

**2. How the Consultant answers.** In any way that works: typing, an AAC device, or a support person writing or typing what the Consultant says,
word for word, at the Consultant's direction. Any question may be skipped. The answers belong to the Consultant; a support person only writes them down.

**3. Payment.** The Authors will pay **[AMOUNT, to be filled in; suggested: an hourly rate for 90 minutes, paid in full whatever time is used]** by
**[PAYMENT METHOD]**. If the Consultant stops at any time, the Consultant is still paid for the time spent. A support person's time is
arranged with the Consultant before the work starts **[SUPPORT-PERSON ARRANGEMENT, if any]**.

**4. How the feedback is used.** The feedback is used to improve the design and the requirements. The Authors will keep a log of each
suggestion and say what change was made, or not made, and why (`FEEDBACK_LOG_TEMPLATE.md`). The papers will report, in summary, how many
requirements changed after review by two paid AAC-user consultants. The Authors will not quote feedback in a way that identifies the Consultant.

**5. No personal data.** The Authors do not ask for, record or analyse anything about the Consultant: no age, health, disability or diagnosis,
no questions about the Consultant's own communication or life, and no audio or video. The Consultant is asked not to write their name or personal stories in the
answers. The feedback is about the system and the materials only. The Authors keep the payment details separately from the feedback, and they
are not published.

**6. Acknowledgement.** The Consultant is not named unless they ask to be, in writing. If they do, the Authors may thank them by name (or by a name they choose)
in a paper. The default is no name.

**7. Stopping.** The Consultant may stop at any time without giving a reason. They may also ask that their feedback is not used, before the
Authors have used it to make a change. After a change has been made from a suggestion, the change stays, but it is not linked to the Consultant.

**8. Independence and limits.** The Consultant speaks only for themselves. The feedback is one person's design advice. It is not a
sample of AAC users, and the Authors will say so. The Authors decide the final design and will explain any suggestion they do not follow.
