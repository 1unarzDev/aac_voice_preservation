## Generative-AI policy
- **SE4All pages [O],[S]: no GenAI policy stated.**
- **IEEE [I] (publisher; applies to IEEE proceedings):** "The use of content generated by artificial intelligence (AI) in an article ... shall be disclosed in the acknowledgments section of any article submitted to an IEEE publication." "The AI system used shall be identified" with the sections and "a brief explanation regarding the level at which the AI system was used to generate the content." Editing/grammar: "disclosure as noted above is not required, but recommended."
- **ICSE 2027 Research Track [R]** (not stated to bind workshops, but same conference): quotes IEEE ("The use of artificial intelligence (AI)-generated text in an article shall be disclosed in the acknowledgements section"; "The sections of the paper that use AI-generated text shall have a citation to the AI system used to generate the text.") and adds: "Submissions will be checked for hallucinated, fabricated, or unverifiable references." Confirmed fake references -> "desk-rejected without review".
- **Consequence for main.tex:** this draft contains AI-generated text. The Acknowledgment must disclose the AI system and which sections it generated (placeholder present, `\pending`). Every reference must be traceable (refs.bib uses only verified entries with URL/DOI; VERIFY-NAMES entries must be checked before submission).
