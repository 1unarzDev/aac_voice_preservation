## Does "model-dependent register default" survive? Yes, as a descriptive statement, with three limits.
Bare V share among address-bearing outputs (n address-bearing outputs in brackets):
| model | DE | FR |
|---|---|---|
| gemma3-4b | 50% (10) | 92% (12) |
| mistral-nemo-12b | 45% (11) | 78% (9) |
| qwen2.5-7b | 45% (11) | 75% (4) |
| llama3.1-8b | 50% (4) | 100% (1) |
| qwen2.5-14b | 25% (4) | 50% (2) |
| gpt-6.1-terra | 7% (14) | 8% (12) |
The pattern is "terra says T, the five open-weight models lean V in FR and are split in DE". Limits: (1) It is carried by one outlier. Terra against the other five pooled: DE 1/14 vs 18/40 V (Fisher p = .011), FR 1/12 vs 23/28 (p < .001; unclustered, post-hoc check). Among the five open-weight models alone, the DE spread is 25 pp (below the 30 pp rule) and the FR spread of 50 pp rests on cells of 1 to 12. (2) Address rate itself varies hugely: share of bare outputs with no address form ranges from 30% (terra DE) to 100% (qwen7b ES) and 95% (llama FR), so each cell is a small conditional sample and the "default" is mostly "no register at all" for most models. (3) The model contrast is confounded by vendor, size, serving stack and the same terra being a labelling model (sol is the primary arbiter; terra labels disclosed). Not size: qwen 7b (45% DE) and 14b (25%) do not separate cleanly and the 12B nemo resembles the 4B gemma. Conclusion: the defensible sentence is "the bare register default differs by model; it is not a language property", not a statement about a population of models.

## Verdict
The frontier's verdict does not change: STOP stays stop, no promotion, no language claim. The 2-model "model-dependent register default" is now met on the declared 6-model rule (range 43 pp DE, 92 pp FR, CI excluding 0) and the control rule, which strengthens it from a 2-model contrast to a 6-model one, but it is mostly terra versus the rest, on small address-bearing cells, with machine labels and translated stimuli. The polarity chip result is now confirmed in 6 of 6 models (mechanism check, positivity-bias replication). New and worth noting: the polarity default off the ceiling (ctxq) is also model-dependent, with an ES language difference that comes from two models.
