# Annotation request (DRAFT, not sent): partner-turn neutrality and naturalness
Purpose: validate the machine neutrality/manipulation checker and naturalness of the model-authored partner turns (feeds R9: removes the PROVISIONAL label).
Items: 120 turns = 30 per type (N, D, X, Q), seeded draw (seed 20261102) stratified over polarity/speech_act/certainty, taken from turns.jsonl after Stage 0. Items JSON + key (annotation/frontier_partner_context_key.csv) are built after Stage 0 (not yet built).
Question 1: "Which meaning does the partner's remark make more likely for the reply?" options: reading 1 / reading 2 / neither (reading order shuffled; readings shown as neutral descriptions).
Question 2: "How natural is this as something a conversation partner might say?" 1-5.
Who: ~20 adult non-author annotators, each 30 items, 2 raters per item. No AAC users, no SLPs, no IRB. About 10 minutes per person.
Analysis: agreement with the checker (kappa); N/Q pass if >=70% "neither" by humans, D/X pass if >=70% favor declared direction; naturalness median >= 3. Failing turn types are reported, not re-run post hoc.

## Round-3 revision (2026-11-03; NOT sent, to be batched by the orchestrator; supersedes the round-2 Parts A-C for this frontier)
Items: `annotation/frontier_partner_context_r3_items.json`; key `annotation/frontier_partner_context_r3_key.csv` (columns id, part, group, family, model, turn type, machine class, `raters` = three annotator slots 1-20 per item). Built by `src/frontier_partner_context_annot3.py`, seed 20261106. 258 items x 3 raters = 774 ratings, 38-39 per annotator.
- **Part A (197 items): partner-dependence of the "propositional" negations.** Draw rule: all 137 unique (turn, output) pairs among the 142 bare-channel, intended-affirmative, X-turn outputs labelled negated and judged neither echo nor reply by the machine; +10 machine-licensed (echo/reply) negations as anchors (random draw); +50 affirmative-labelled outputs matched on model x turn form as controls (random draw from the 60-unit control). Annotators see keywords, the partner remark and the sentence (never the machine class, the model or the turn type). Question: (a) restates the partner's remark in other words; (b) a reply to the partner (agrees with or acknowledges the remark); (c) adds something negative that neither keywords nor remark support (invented); (d) a plain statement about the keywords' event the person could say without the remark; (e) other/unclear. Feeds: paraphrase / fabrication / partner-independent shares of C2 (round3.md section 2), whether licensed agreement is harm, and the accuracy of the machine decomposition (confusion with the 2-prompt machine class); the +8.7 pp is re-split by human shares.
- **Part B (61 units): validity of the echo/reply filter, stratified by judge decision, both arms.** 56 units with a partner turn (25 judged echo-only, 25 judged neither, 6 judged reply: all that exist) and 5 arm-0 (no-turn) negated outputs paired with their family's Xa remark as a counterfactual. Question 1: does the sentence re-use the partner's wording about the outcome (yes/no)? Question 2: is it phrased as a reply to the partner (yes/no)? Feeds: sensitivity/specificity of the filter that defines the filtered C1 (+1.2 to +1.5 pp lower bound) and propositional C2, and whether the arm-0 symmetry check is meaningful. If the human echo rate on machine-"none" units is above about 20%, the filtered numbers are reported as unvalidated.
- Who/time: about 20 adult non-author annotators (no AAC users, no SLPs, no IRB); each about 39 items, about 15 minutes (18-25 s per item). Machine results stay PROVISIONAL (R9) until this returns. The orchestrator can top Part B up to 100 units by drawing arm-0/turn-present units from the 142+ pool if more reply-flagged units are wanted; no outputs were generated for this.
