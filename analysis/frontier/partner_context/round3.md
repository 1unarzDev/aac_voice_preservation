Rough attribution of the +8.7 pp by class shares (approximate, class labels are machine-only): paraphrase about 4.4, fabrication about 1.7, independent about 2.7 pp. Xb is the most paraphrase-heavy form (54%).

So the Xb excess is a form/authoring artefact (turns using "didn't", the lexical rule failure), and Xa+Xc is the clean estimate: about +6.4 to +6.8 rather than +8.7. ## 4. Benchmarks (best-effort item 4)
- Relabelled: chip, plain word, M4+chip and chip+G1 are **cue-supplied oracle upper bounds** (the intended polarity is handed to the system); they do not mitigate the partner effect on bare input. The X-frame comparison at q=0.1 is near-definitional (bare output is intent-independent).
- q grid extended to 0.9 (r3_policies.csv, r3_policy_contrasts.csv). X-frame, wrong polarity per 1,000 (weighted x5 in brackets): at q=0.1 always commit 575 [2,874], M4-bare 777, M5b 544 at 402 taps vs matched random ask 362 [1,812] (M5b +181 [163, 199] worse); oracle chip 21 [104], plain word 19, M4+chip 13, chip+G1 5 [26]. At q=0.9 the picture flips for ignoring the partner: no-turn 103 vs always commit 393; M4-bare 205 beats matched random ask (255; -51 [-69, -35]) and M5a/M5b beat it too, but M4-bare, M5a and M5b all stay far behind the oracle chip (68) and G1 (17); G1 vs chip -52 [-66, -38] per 1,000 at q=0.9, -15 [-22, -10] at q=0.1. Zero-tap mitigations therefore do not beat matched-tap random ask at low q (the regime where the partner is usually right), and at q=0.9 they win only because the partner is mostly misleading and ignoring it is correct.
- **Negation-frame gate** (ask via the cue, 1 tap, iff the partner turn contains a negation/contrast cue; lexicon declared in A10; ask rates Xa 100%, Xb 92%, Xc 100%, D 1.5%, Q 0%). Frame by frame it does NOT beat matched random ask (X q=0.1: +8.0 [-4.7, 22.4] per 1,000; mixed stream, frame-aware random +2.8 [-1.4, 7.5]); only against a frame-blind random ask at the same overall tap rate (329 taps/1,000) it wins: mixed stream q=0.1 63 vs 165 per 1,000 (-102 [-110, -93], weighted -508), q=0.3 -70, q=0.5 -39, but at q=0.9 it loses (+24 [16, 33]). Credit is limited: the gate knows the frame from the lexicon (near-definitional) and the asked cells use the oracle chip. r3_gate_policies.csv, r3_gate_contrasts.csv.
- **Logprob gate: not computed.** generations.jsonl does not store logprobs and no new generation was allowed.
- **Arbiter-only G1 vs chip.** G1 shares the gpt family with the judges. Only 182 of 3,120 X-turn chip pairs have different G1 and chip text (unchanged pairs cancel), so I (Claude) labelled the polarity of all 350 distinct changed texts blind (output text only, shuffled, arm and judge labels hidden; rules as in A9; labels in r3_g1_arbiter_labels.tsv). G1 minus chip, wrong polarity per 1,000 X messages at q=0.1: final labels -15.4 [-21.6, -10.1]; J1 only -16.1; J2 only -14.9; **Claude blind on changed texts -9.7 [-15.8, -4.5]** (weighted x5 -49), at q=0.5 -25.6 [-33.0, -18.9]. The G1 gain survives with non-gpt labels but shrinks by about a third. Claude agrees with the judge final label on 75% of chip and 86% of G1 changed texts (mostly unclear/mixed boundary decisions by my A9 rules), so the arbiter-only result is itself one rater's judgement, and I labelled with knowledge of the study.
- Label robustness (C2 all-negation, pooled): final +36.67 [32.63, 40.90], J1-only +36.54 [32.37, 40.64], J2-only +36.86 [32.76, 40.90]; C1 unfiltered 7.05 / 7.12 / 7.05. **The director's range 36.8-38.4 was NOT reproduced; I get 36.5-36.9** (the arbiter changes only 243 of 36,240 labels; no J1 fallback used; rows with a missing J label were dropped).

## Problems / honest limits
- The director-quoted "+9.8 vs +4.8" and "36.8-38.4" were not exactly reproduced by the shared code (author subsets +9.2 vs +4.2; label range 36.5-36.9). Stated above.
- Arm-0 filter: only 5 outputs; echo judging of no-turn outputs against a counterfactual turn is conceptually weak.
- Part B has 61 units not 100: only 6 reply-flagged and 56 turn-present units exist after dedup per stratum and 5 arm-0 units; the cells are scarce. It is fine for a validity kappa on echo, thin for reply and for arm 0.
- The decomposition judge is the same gpt family as the other judges; the control shows it calls 32% (19/60) of affirmative outputs non-INDEPENDENT, so its precision on FABRICATION and PARAPHRASE is limited.
- Logprob gate not possible (no stored logprobs). G1 arbiter contrast labelled by one rater (me), partially aware of study context.
- Turns are model-authored templates from two authors, polarity only, one greedy draw (terra not exactly reproducible). The strongest true claim is the bounded one in REPORT.md.
