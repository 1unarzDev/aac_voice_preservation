## FATAL
1. **"G1 is harmful for politeness (measured)" (Headline 3) is a construction artefact.** `interventions.check` flags `politeness:added` whenever `spec.polite_ok` is false and the output has a polite marker (source lines 152-153). The stored inputs are bare keywords (all 7,200 rows begin "Keywords:"), so spec is empty and the flag fires on any polite output by design. The "100% of correct polite outputs flagged" is therefore tautological (my crosstab: 952 of 984 source-polite, polite-output rows flagged, not exactly 100%; the rest is regex mismatch). This tests G1 outside its design (guard with a supplied cue spec), not G1 as deployed. "Repaired 0 dropped negations" is likewise by construction (empty spec cannot name a dropped cue), yet the same-items table labels it "(measured)" and reports it as an intervention result with 0 avoided. The "+100.6 worst case" row (150.1 reversals, -100.6 avoided) is a hypothetical in which regeneration strips politeness; no regeneration was run, and it is placed in the same table as measured rows with CIs. Fix: relabel as "G1 inapplicable to bare keywords (no spec) / unmeasured", drop "harmful" and "measured", move the worst case out of the comparison table or flag it visually as an assumption, and state that the real guard test needs a supplied cue (already in analysis/guard_baseline).

## MAJOR
2. **Interventions table mixes measured and assumed numbers.** Chip, plain word and "chip on every message" use rho transported from hw1 pairs (chip 0.995, word 0.987, question/request ~0.79-0.9) and assume asking always yields the cue. "Chip on every message removes nearly all polarity reversals (0.1 left)" is simply (1 - rho) x 23.9, not an outcome. Stated, but the CIs shown ignore rho uncertainty (only a "[rho lower CI]" row at 5%), and the headline benefit "avoided vs commit" is then an arithmetic transformation of the policy ranking. Chip vs plain word "interchangeable" is just rho 0.995 vs 0.987 with no test.
3. **Prior point estimates are not centered in their bootstrap distributions.** Polarity prior vs gated random: diff -4.2, CI [-9.0, +2.2], but p = 0.64 (about 32% of replicates >= 0 on the minority side), and prior vs confidence: diff -0.4 with CI [-4.4, +7.7]. The point estimate comes from the full-sample outer CV while replicates refit on resamples with a smaller effective n, so the reported "relative reduction 20% [-11, 42]" is biased optimistic relative to the sampling distribution it is paired with. Report percentile CI with bias, or use a .632-style/out-of-bag evaluation. Plan said B = 300 for refit policies; B = 1000 was run (summary_r2.json) with the script default 500: harmless but an undeclared change.
4. **Section 6 and Headline 2 still sound like findings.** "Prior removes 6.8 [0.3, 11.9] per 1,000 (29%)" is versus always-commit, not a declared contrast, lower bound 0.3; "asks per reversal avoided: prior 14.7 vs random 38.1" divides by a quantity whose CI touches 0, and the two are not different per the primary contrasts. Headline 2 spotlights qwen2.5-7b (-7.0, q = .018), the best of three single models chosen after seeing results within a 212-contrast exploratory family; it should say "selected post hoc, exploratory". Also the model-visible event averages the same four models whose confidence is the policy (qwen2.5-7b confidence ranks units by the very model whose misses define the event): a circularity that favours single-model confidence and makes the qwen result non-transferable to a different generator.
5. **Weak/invalid comparators remain.** 4 of 9 probe cells fail the declared validity rule (gemma negation AUROC .57 [.47, .67]; all politeness), yet "confidence, probe mean" averages invalid cells in and is the primary comparator (P2, P4). Politeness primary contrasts therefore compare the prior to an invalid comparator; the declared closest published method (CLAM-style ambiguity classifier) was not implemented ("entropy" is explicitly a stand-in), so the benchmark-against-prior-method requirement is unmet and the novelty claim over CLAM cannot be assessed. 3 of 5 probe models; verbal arm not run (38 strings).
6. **Power.** 76 polarity exposure units (about 43 effective at the model-visible rate): TOST margin 5/1,000 cannot be met; all primary contrasts are "not distinguishable". Publishable only as a null/measurement paper; the abstract must not imply the prior helps. Holm family of 4 is fine, but the 212-contrast exploratory family with 15 BH hits (12 on the upper-bound exposure event) invites cherry-picking.
7. **Acceptability comparison is not interpretable and shouldn't appear beside the tables.** guard_original 0/20 are selected judge-failed items (acceptable = 0 by selection), arms have 20-40 rater-items from 2 raters on different items; "chip/word acceptable more than guard repairs" has no test and is confounded by selection. Keep, but label as not comparable in the table caption itself.
8. **Speech act/politeness remain detector-proxy labels** (provisional, correctly marked). The model-visible event rates (e.g. 161.9 question/request per 1,000) are driven by a regex-based source label; SLP weights from two raters (NEG 1.0, ACT 0.8, POL 0.5) are descriptive and the plan's "weight x reversals" cross-dimension sum treats unlike events as commensurable.
9. **H1 belief-scale reading.** Gaps of +35-46 pp on polarity (q about 37-49% vs 2.7%) from a yes/no probe are largely acquiescence/prompt artefact (the report says so); the "profile gap present" rule is mechanically satisfied (all q near 0.4) and informative only as "probes are poor base-rate estimators". Not a finding about AAC; BSS < 0 is the real content. The stored greedy-vs-posterior table is correctly labelled.

## Novelty (search 2026-11-02)
Targeted AAC search found no AAC-specific prior on negation-dropping in keyword expansion with clarification. Closest real work: Chen et al. ACL 2023 "Say What You Mean! LLMs speak too positively about negative commonsense knowledge" (keyword-to-sentence negation failure, already cited), Kuhn et al. CLAM (ambiguity classifier + clarify), and a NAACL 2025 Findings pipeline deciding which examples to clarify (arXiv/ACL titles only, not opened). Novelty is the measurement framework (human keyword base rates, SLP-weighted reversals per 1,000, taps), not the ask policy; the policy result is a null and must be framed so.

## Strongest true claim
On 1,800 human keyword units, a model-visible polarity reversal rate of about 24 per 1,000 messages (negation), 49 (politeness) and 162 (question/request) under always-commit; no tested ask policy (keyword prior, model confidence, entropy, shortest-first) is distinguishable from gated-random at a 10% ask budget after Holm, and probe-model forced-choice beliefs are poorly calibrated to human base rates (BSS < 0 on polarity).
