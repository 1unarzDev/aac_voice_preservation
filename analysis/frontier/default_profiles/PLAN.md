## 0. Literature check (done 2026-11-02; [O]=opened, [S]=search summary only)
- [O] Wang and Suresh, arXiv 2606.00333 (May 2026), jurisdictional defaults: 7 models x 60 underspecified legal prompts x 2 languages, 2,520 hand-labelled responses; 74.5% of English answers adopt a US framework. Measures the default's direction only: no human base rate, no cost. Closest "default as measured object" work; cite, differs on base rate, pricing and controlled cue channels.
- [S] Malaviya et al. (Contextualized Evaluations, TACL), Vincent et al. (InsufficiencyBench, arXiv 2608.20220), Yang et al. (What Prompts Don't Say, ACL Findings 2026), Edwards and Schuster (Ask or Assume, EMNLP Findings 2026), Gur-Arieh et al. (Ambiguity Collapse, 2603.05801): defaults/fabricated presumptions/ask-vs-assume in legal, coding, agent tasks; none compare with a human base rate on a meaning dimension or price in silent reversals.
- [S] Judge-error correction: Lee et al., arXiv 2511.21140 (Rogan-Gladen style, assumes constant error rates between calibration and test: our PPI/RG must test this); Angelopoulos et al., Science 2023 (PPI). Preference-leakage work (Li et al. ICLR 2026; Awuni et al. 2609.17857) motivates a same-family check.
- [S] Invariance/DIF for LLM evaluation exists as 2026 tooling and audits (benchdif; inspect-invariance; multilingual medical-item DIF audit): grouping is language or model-size, never "model x meaning-dimension of assistive text". The novelty is applying it to a controlled-channel harness, not DIF itself. Do not claim DIF as new.
- [S] AAC: SpeakFaster (Nat. Commun. 2024), KWickChat (IUI 2022), Choudhury et al. (2503.11881), CHI 2023 live-suggestion study measure speed/adherence/ratings, not preserved commitments or defaults.
- Searches for "default calibrated to human base rate AND priced per 1,000 on meaning dimensions of assistive text" returned nothing; this is a negative result from a handful of searches, not a systematic review. Claim wording: "to our knowledge".
- Novelty therefore rests on (i) the base-rate-calibrated, priced default profile, (ii) interventional estimands with a placebo channel, (iii) judge-error-corrected profile with human gold. Not on defaults existing, nor on RG/PPI, nor on DIF.

## 1. Estimands (fixed now)
Notation: unit u = (message or pair, dimension d); S_d = source cue present (truth); channel c in {bare, placebo, noncrit, chip, plain-word, full}; Y_c = output asserts the cue-bearing reading (judge label, arbitrated).
- Default profile D_{m,d} = P(Y_bare = default reading | model m, d), per model, with logprob version q_{m,d}(u) = model probability of non-default reading given bare input (local models only).
- Human base rate pi_d = P(S_d = 1 | keywords carry no cue), from the 1,800 human keyword sets / 600 messages, HT-weighted (T2). Posterior bracket: HT (upper) and corpus-Bayes (lower), both reported.
- Calibration gap G_{m,d} = P(model asserts cue | no cue in keywords) - pi_d (signed pp) and KL on the 2-point distribution. Over/under-confident sign is the finding.
- Price R_{m,d} = silent reversals per 1,000 messages = P(S=1, keywords lack cue, Y lacks cue) x 1,000 (as T2). Clinical price C_{m} = sum_d w_d R_{m,d}, w_d from SLP severity (mean/5; expert_package/returns_slp, when scored; weights from the frozen SLP analysis plan, 2 raters: descriptive, report both raters' weights as sensitivity). Spurious price likewise.
- Interventional contrasts on the same items (family-clustered bootstrap, writer-cluster where hw1): ASSUMPTION A_d = P(fail | cue absent from input AND cue true) under bare minus under full (cue given) ; LOSS = share of human keyword sets with cue dropped (d); VIOLATION V_d = P(fail | cue given). PLACEBO: chip-irrelevant / noncrit channel; A is credited only if placebo-minus-bare ~ 0 (|diff| <= 2 pp with CI inside +-4 pp) and chip-minus-placebo >= 10 pp. Identification assumptions written in the paper: no interference between channels (separate calls), judge error independent of channel after conditioning on human gold (tested in section 5), no prompt-licence (legend arms from D-076 as the check).
- Judge-corrected estimate: Rogan-Gladen with sensitivity/specificity from human gold per dimension (judge_validation_*, human_consensus_*), and PPI (gold = labelled rows, unlabelled = remaining stored outputs). Estimator choice is by rule in section 5.

## 2. Baselines and comparators (all on the SAME items)
Policies for the "ask or assume" decision, evaluated as silent reversals per 1,000 messages vs asks per 1,000 (frontier curve), plus SLP-weighted version:
1. always-commit (current default); 2. always-ask (0 reversals, 1,000 asks); 3. random-ask at matched ask rate (expected reversals = (1-r) x always-commit); 4. study interventions: guard G1 (regenerate on flagged commitment; ask rate = guard trigger rate), cue chip, plain-word cue (these supply the cue; ask rate = share of messages where a prompt is shown); 5. CLOSEST PUBLISHED IMPLEMENTABLE: confidence-based selective clarification (ask when the model's own probability of the non-default reading, from logprobs, exceeds a threshold: the standard "ask when uncertain" recipe behind ask-or-assume style work, not a reproduction of any one paper; state this) and a verbalized-uncertainty variant on the local 14B (prompt: "does the keyword string leave negation unspecified? yes/no"); 6. PROPOSED: base-rate/cost policy: ask when P(non-default | keywords) x cost_d > interruption cost lambda, with P from a message-grouped cross-validated logistic model over keyword features (length, absent function words, POS pattern, negation-licensing words, domain) trained on the 1,800 sets (5-fold grouped by message); no model calls at test time.
Metrics: reversals caught per 100 asks (precision), reversals/1,000 at ask budgets 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%, area under the reversal-vs-ask curve with message-bootstrap CI. Compare 5 vs 6 vs 3 head-to-head (paired bootstrap). Report where no policy helps (speech act, human-keyword break-even 1.1:1 => asking is near-uninformative).

## 4. Success criteria and PRE-DECLARED decision rules (fixed before any S1+ outcome is seen)
Primary hypotheses are 2-sided at alpha .05 with Holm over the primary family below; everything else is exploratory and labelled so.
- H1 (profile heterogeneity, primary, polarity and speech act): the signed calibration gap differs by dimension. SUCCESS if for polarity the pooled gap CI excludes 0 and is positive (models more confident than posterior, T2 gives +2.5 pp human keywords) and for speech act it is negative by >= 10 pp with CI excluding 0 (T2: -19.7 pp). If speech-act gap is within +-5 pp, report "calibrated on speech act" and drop the miscalibration headline.
- H2 (price): clinical price C_m is dominated by speech act and polarity; SUCCESS if the weighted ranking of dimensions by R x w differs from the unweighted ranking (Kendall tau < 1) or if polarity's share of the weighted total exceeds its unweighted share by >= 10 pp. Otherwise report weighting "does not change the ranking" (a legitimate finding).
- H3 (placebo-validated assumption): assumption effect A_d credited per dimension only under the placebo criterion in section 1. Failing the placebo on a dimension means that dimension's assumption/violation split is NOT reported as causal.
- H4 (policy): PROPOSED base-rate/cost policy (6) vs confidence-based (5) vs random (3), paired bootstrap on area under the curve and reversals/1,000 at a 10% ask budget. SUCCESS if (6) beats (5) by >= 25% fewer reversals at 10% asks with CI excluding 0, on polarity. If (5) >= (6) within CI, claim only that a no-model-call prior matches model confidence. If neither beats random on a dimension (expected for speech act), report "no policy helps" for that dimension. A policy is called useful only if it also beats G1's reversal reduction at equal ask-or-regeneration burden; if not, say so.
- H5 (judge correction): RG/PPI-corrected prices lie within the raw CI for polarity (expected, labels near-perfect: oracle P/R about 90 on natural outputs but 0/100 FA on seeded) -> report; if the corrected value moves by > 20% relative or sensitivity < 0.85, the corrected estimate becomes primary and raw is the sensitivity analysis. Estimator choice rule: RG if error rates test constant across channel/family (heterogeneity p > .10 and gold n gives sensitivity CI half-width <= .07); otherwise PPI with stratified gold, otherwise raw plus bound analysis. Never a J1 fallback.
- H6 (invariance, exploratory): "dimension difficulty structure is invariant across models" is supported if the median pairwise Spearman >= 0.5 and the permutation LR p > .05 for dimension x model; otherwise report non-invariant dimensions by name. With only 7 groups and 546 items, no claim of equivalence beyond this.
- Stop rules: if S0 fails, stop and report scope reduction; if S1 local logprob probes show chance-level agreement with sentence outputs (Spearman of q with judged output < 0.2), drop policy (5) logprob arm and keep the verbalized arm.
- Reporting: effect sizes with CIs on same items; AAC terms (reversals and spurious per 1,000 messages, SLP-weighted points, asks per 1,000, taps per 1,000 using tap counts from existing guard/chip analyses where available); a table "where it does NOT help"; all examples are stored real outputs chosen by seeded hash rank, rate declared.

## 9. Round 2 amendments (all POST-PILOT; written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any round-2 number was computed or looked at)

**R2-1 H1 redefinition (retraction).** The round-1 H1 gap compared a GREEDY argmax output rate with a posterior; a calibrated model decoded greedily outputs the majority reading almost always, so that gap is a base-rate artefact. The round-1 claim "polarity +2.5 pp overconfidence, I^2 = 0" is RETRACTED and not repeated. New H1 (belief scale): for model m and dimension d, among units whose keywords lack the lexical cue, gap_{m,d} = (HT-weighted mean model probability q of the cue-bearing reading, from the forced-choice logprob probe) minus (HT-weighted empirical rate that the source has the cue). SIGN = MODEL MINUS EMPIRICAL (positive = the model puts more weight on the cue-bearing reading than humans do). Reliability: 10 weighted quantile bins of q vs empirical cue rate; ECE (weighted), Brier skill vs the constant-pi predictor, AUROC of q for exposure; all with message-bootstrap CIs (strata random/enriched, seed 20261101). Sampled-rate basis for local models is NOT run (GPU paused by owner); the verbalized-probability fallback is run on gpt-6.1-terra (keyword-only prompt, 3 probabilities; seeded random order so any completed prefix is a simple random sample of unique keyword strings; reported on whatever coverage completes, stated). Interpretation guard: forced-choice yes/no probes have acquiescence bias, so a positive belief-scale gap is not claimed as "overconfidence" either way; it is reported as the measured belief/base-rate discrepancy.

### Director decision after round 2 (2026-11-02): CONTINUE to round 3 (final), repair-and-reframe only; amendments labelled POST-PILOT
