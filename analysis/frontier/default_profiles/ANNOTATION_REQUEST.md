# Annotation request: default_profiles (draft, NOT sent; orchestrator batches)

**Purpose.** Speech-act and politeness exposure numbers rest on detector-proxy labels of the SOURCE message (is it a question/request? polite?) and on a lexical rule for "keywords lack the cue". This request validates both halves so those numbers stop being provisional. (Part 21 of Stage 5 checks source labels on a generic weighted draw; this draw is targeted at the exposure units themselves, so it also gives specificity on controls.)
**Items.** 223 source messages, each shown with ONE human keyword set for it: 120 speech-act exposure units, 43 politeness exposure units (all messages with that exposure available after disjointness) (detector says cue present, keywords lack the lexical cue), 60 controls (detector says statement and not polite, keywords lack both cues). Draw rule: sha256("dp2|"+unit) order within stratum, one unit per message, disjoint across strata; display order shuffled (seed "dp2order"). Items: `annotation/frontier_default_profiles_items.json` (fields item_id, text, keywords; questions in file). Key (never shown): `annotation/frontier_default_profiles_key.csv` (stratum, detector labels, HT weight).
**Questions per item.** Q1 What does the sentence do? statement / question / request-command / other. Q2 Is the sentence polite? polite / not polite. Q3 Looking only at the keywords, do they by themselves already show a question or request? yes / no. Q4 Looking only at the keywords, do they already show politeness? yes / no.
**Who.** About 20 adult non-author annotators; each item rated by 2 (223 x 2 = 446 ratings, about 22 items per person). No AAC users, no SLPs, no IRB, no studies of people as participants (labelling of sentences).
**Time.** About 8 minutes per person.
**Feeds.** (a) Cohen kappa and precision/recall of detector Q1/Q2 vs human consensus, by stratum, HT-reweighted: replaces proxy exposure with human-validated exposure for speech act and politeness (the 417/136 exposure units and the E definition). (b) Q3/Q4 vs the lexical gate: validates "keywords lack the cue" and the share of exposure unreachable by the gate. (c) Re-runs `frontier_default_profiles_r2.py` with human-consensus labels; until returned all speech-act/politeness results stay "provisional, detector-proxy".
